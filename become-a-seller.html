Questions

Before you apply

What can I sell?

Source code, website templates, scripts, plugins, APIs, software, Minecraft resources, game assets, UI kits and graphics — as long as the files match the category you choose.

Why was my product rejected?

Every rejection carries a reason such as files not matching the description, quality below standard, an incomplete package, or a licence issue. You can fix the product and resubmit.

How is the commission calculated?

40% of the sale by default, stored on the transaction at the moment it is paid. Your share is written to the ledger in the same operation, so the balance always matches the sales.

Do buyers get the files immediately?

Yes. A paid order creates ownership, and the library issues a signed download link that expires after two minutes.

Can I sell the same product elsewhere?

You choose the licence you offer here. Aera Market only requires that the product is yours to sell and that the files match what the listing claims.
